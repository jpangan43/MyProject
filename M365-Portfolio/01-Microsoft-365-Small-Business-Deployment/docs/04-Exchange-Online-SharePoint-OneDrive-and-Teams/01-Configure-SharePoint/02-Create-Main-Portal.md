# Create Main Portal

Simple step-by-step guide to create the main company communication site (portal) in SharePoint.

## Steps

### 1 – Create active sites
In the SharePoint admin center, go to **Active sites** and click **Create**.

![Step 1](../extracted/MainPortal/Create%20Main%20Portal/1%20-%20Create%20active%20sites.png)

---

### 2 – Choose communication site
Select **Communication site**.

![Step 2](../extracted/MainPortal/Create%20Main%20Portal/2-%20Choose%20communication%20site.png)

---

### 3 – Choose Standard communication template
Select the **Standard communication** template.

![Step 3](../extracted/MainPortal/Create%20Main%20Portal/3%20-%20Choose%20Standard%20communication%20template.png)

---

### 4 – Click use template
Click **Use template**.

![Step 4](../extracted/MainPortal/Create%20Main%20Portal/4%20-%20Click%20use%20template.png)

---

### 5 – Enter the details for your site
Enter site name, address, and description (e.g. Company Portal).

![Step 5](../extracted/MainPortal/Create%20Main%20Portal/5%20-%20Enter%20the%20details%20for%20your%20site.png)

---

### 6 – Create site
Click **Create site**.

![Step 6](../extracted/MainPortal/Create%20Main%20Portal/6%20-%20Create%20site.png)

---

### 7 – Communication site created
Confirm the communication site (main portal) has been created.

![Step 7](../extracted/MainPortal/Create%20Main%20Portal/7%20-%20Communication%20site%20created.png)

---

## Notes

- Communication sites are ideal for company-wide portals and news.
- After creation, register this site as a **Hub site** so department sites can associate with it.
