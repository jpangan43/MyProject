# Create the Department Sites

Simple step-by-step guide to create department Team sites and register the Company Portal as a hub site.

## Steps

### 1 – Create Team Site
Start creating a new Team site for a department.


---

### 2 – Create Standard Team
Choose the **Standard team** option.


---

### 3 – Use Template
Select and use the template.


---

### 4 – Enter site details
Enter the department site name, address, and description.


---

### 5 – Create site
Click **Create site**.


---

### 6 – Add site owners and members
Add owners and members for the department site.


---

### 7 – List of active department sites
Confirm the department sites appear in the active sites list.


---

### 8 – JasVA Company Portal
View the main Company Portal site.


---

### 9 – Sample Department Site
Example of a completed department site.


---

### 10 – Register Company Portal as Hub site
Register the Company Portal as a **Hub site**.


---

### 11 – Company Portal registered as hub site
Confirm the Company Portal is registered as a hub site.

![Step 11](../extracted/DeptSites/Create%20the%20Department%20Sites/11%20-%20Company%20Portal%20registeredas%20hub%20site.png)

---

## Notes

- Create one Team site per department.
- Associate department sites with the Company Portal hub for navigation and branding.
- Assign owners and members according to department structure.
